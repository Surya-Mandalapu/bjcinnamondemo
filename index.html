Baked with Heart in Folsom

BJ Cinnamon is a family-owned bakery dedicated to crafting the finest morning treats. Since taking ownership, Sochanthoeun To and Vearady Chea have poured their passion into making the bakery a staple of the Folsom community.

From early dawn, our ovens are warm and fryers are ready, producing sweet aromas that draw lines out the door by 5:00 AM. We believe that a great donut or cinnamon roll has the power to brighten your entire day.
